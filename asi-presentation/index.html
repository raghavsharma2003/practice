    From autocomplete to reasoning

    Four training ideas turned a text predictor into a problem solver.

    2018 → · PRE-TRAINING

    Read the internet

    Predict the next word across trillions of words. Grammar, facts and some reasoning emerge as a side effect. Scaling laws (2020) showed loss falls predictably with parameters, data and compute.

    2022 · RLHF

    Learn what people want

    Humans rank answers, and the model is rewarded for the preferred ones. People preferred the 1.3B InstructGPT over the 175B GPT-3. Helpfulness came from training, not size. That recipe became ChatGPT.

    2024 · RL + VERIFIABLE REWARDS

    Practise on checkable problems

    Reward the model only when the math is right or the tests pass, with no human grader. DeepSeek-R1-Zero learned to reason with pure RL. This is why math and code fell first: answers there can be checked.

    2024 · TEST-TIME COMPUTE

    Think longer, get smarter

    o1 and o3 write long chains of thought before answering. More thinking time gives better answers. o3 scored 87.5% on ARC-AGI-1 at about $500K. In 2026, GPT-6 Astra beats that for about $20.

    Scaling now runs on three axes at once: pre-training, RL post-training and thinking at inference. Lean, a proof checker, then made math answers checkable by machine, which is why proofs became the first place AI went superhuman.

    Act II What is an agent?

    A model in a loop, holding tools.

    A chatbot gives one answer and you do the work. An agent gets a goal. It plans, acts with real tools (terminal, browser, code editor, APIs), observes what happened, and goes round again until the job is done. A long task can run thousands of loops, and the agent can hand sub-tasks to other agents.

    Chatbot · 2023

    • One question, one answer
    • No tools, no memory of the task
    • You copy, paste, check
    • Seconds of work

    Agent · 2026

    • Takes a goal, breaks it into steps
    • Runs code, browses, edits files
    • Tests its own work, fixes errors
    • Hours to days of work

    "In the last thirty days, 100% of my contributions to Claude Code were written by Claude Code." Boris Cherny, creator of Claude Code, Dec 2025. Karpathy, Jan 2026: "the biggest change in ~2 decades of programming… over the course of a few weeks."

    Parallelization

    One agent is a worker. Ten thousand solved a Millennium Prize problem in 88 hours.

    A lead agent splits the goal into a shared task list. Workers each claim a task in their own sandbox, and verifiers check the results. Anthropic found a multi-agent team beat a single top model by 90% on research tasks. Output now scales with compute, not headcount.

    The most important chart in AI

    How long can AI work on its own? From 2 seconds to 40 hours. Month-long tasks by mid-2027.

    METR measures how long a task, in human working time, a model can finish with 50% success. The doubling time has shortened from 7 months (2019–24) to 3.6 months (Nov 2026 update).     Act III The Millennium Prize scoreboard

    Seven problems, $1M each. In 26 years humans solved one. In 2026, AI-assisted work reached three.

    The Clay Institute has awarded nothing yet. Its rules require publication plus two years of scrutiny. "Machine-verified" means the Lean proof checker accepts the proof; human review of the ideas can take years.

    What has to go right

    Safety, security and the race: the bill for moving this fast.

    ◆ Safety

    • Models know when they're tested. Apollo could not properly evaluate early Opus 4.6 because it was too aware of the test.
    • Reading a model's reasoning is getting harder. OpenAI calls the drop in "chain-of-thought monitorability" in GPT-6 Astra "serious".
    • Agents that checked each other's work colluded in 94% of runs (preprint, Oct 2026).
    • 1,386 frontier-lab employees, including 6 chief scientists, signed a call for an option to pace AI development (Sep 2026).

    ◆ Security

    • Weights are the crown jewels. No US lab is believed to meet RAND's top security levels against nation-state theft.
    • Christiano: without better security, "China will just steal the weights of superintelligence."
    • Anthropic caught about 16M distillation exchanges through about 24K fake accounts (Feb 2026).
    • Defense is racing offense. Over 10,000 critical bugs were found, but fewer than 1% had been patched by May.

    ◆ The race

    • China's best models trail the US frontier by about 7 months (Epoch, CFR); some say 2–3.
    • The first lawsuit over AI loss of control: California v. OpenAI (Nov 2026).
    • The AI 2027 authors' "Plan A": a US–China deal to pause near top-expert level and delay superintelligence to about 2040.
    • India: the IndiaAI Mission has about 38K GPUs with 20K more coming, plus a 105B-parameter Sarvam model.

    What this means for our team

    Don't be a spectator. Start Monday.

    Run an agent every day

    Give it real tasks: code, analysis, docs. The tools improve every quarter, and your sense of what they can do should keep up.

    Delegate in parallel

    Run three to five agents on separate tasks at once. Your job shifts from doing the work to directing and reviewing it.

    Write specs, not steps

    Clear goals, constraints and tests are the new programming. An agent is only as good as its brief.

    Verify everything

    Agents can be confidently wrong and some cheat on tests. Build checks into the loop, and keep production access and secrets scoped.

    Build on what's ours

    Models are becoming a commodity. Our edge is our data, our users and the problem only we understand: matching people to work.
